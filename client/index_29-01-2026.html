About

Madhavaneni Raghunandan Rao

Born into a respected Telangana family, Madhavaneni Raghunandan Rao grew up with a strong sense of justice, integrity, and responsibility. His childhood was shaped by values rooted in honesty, discipline, and service to society.

Growing up in Medak region, he witnessed the struggles of rural families, farmers, and youth — experiences that shaped his lifelong commitment to public service.
