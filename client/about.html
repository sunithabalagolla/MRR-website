Early Roots Born in Siddipet, shaped by humility and Telangana’s values.
Strong Foundation. Degrees in Science, Law, Education & Human Rights — a rare blend of intellect.
Early Public Insight Patancheru & journalism experience deepened his understanding of people’s struggles.

Early Life & Foundations of Leadership

  • Born on 23 March 1968 in Siddipet, rooted in Telangana’s cultural and social fabric.
  • Rose from a humble, self-made background, symbolizing determination and discipline.
  • Completed academic qualifications in Science, Law, Education, and Human Rights—a rare combination reflecting intellectual breadth.
  • Early years in Patancheru and a brief career in journalism sharpened his understanding of grassroots challenges.
  • Developed a strong commitment to truth, justice, and constitutional values, shaping his lifelong public ethos.

Legal Excellence & Public Voice

  • Built a widely respected career as a High Court lawyer known for clarity in argument and fearless advocacy.
  • Earned recognition for defending citizens’ rights with unwavering integrity.
  • Became a public intellectual during the Telangana movement, interpreting law through the lens of people’s welfare.
  • Known for razor-sharp logic, bold communication, and a principled approach that resonated across communities.
  • His legal activism positioned him as a truth-driven voice capable of challenging entrenched systems.

Transformative Political Breakthrough

  • Achieved a historic win in the 2020 Dubbak by-poll, reshaping Telangana’s political landscape.
  • Defied political machinery and conventional equations through grassroots trust and credibility.
  • Emerged as a symbol of:
    1. Integrity triumphing over power
    2. Truth defeating propaganda
    3. People’s faith defeating political influence
  • His victory energized the opposition narrative in Telangana and cemented him as a leader with both courage and moral authority.

Parliamentarian with Purpose

  • Elected as Member of Parliament, Lok Sabha (Medak), in 2024.
  • Represents Telangana’s aspirations at the national level with clarity, conviction, and constitutional grounding.
  • Focus areas include:
    1. Farmers’ welfare & rural transformation
    2. Youth empowerment & employment
    3. Women’s safety & dignity
    4. Transparent governance & accountability
    5. Preservation of Telangana’s cultural identity and pride
  • Known for articulating issues with legal depth, moral clarity, and a firm people-first approach.

Values That Define Him

  • Fearless communicator with a reputation for speaking truth without compromise.
  • Believes in clean, principled politics rooted in service, not power.
  • Views public office as a sacred responsibility, not a privilege.
  • Inspires citizens through his discipline, integrity, and commitment to justice.
  • Embodies leadership driven by:
    1. Courage
    2. Conviction
    3. Constitutional integrity
    4. Service to people above all else
